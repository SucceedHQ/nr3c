Design logic
The mark
A clock face with exactly 5 tick marks — one for each tool. Hands sit at 10:10, the classic "smile" position used in watch advertising.
The name
"Vive" — Latin and French for "alive." Suggests a productivity tool that keeps your day moving. Short, memorable, distinct.
